Lore

The drift

I

Nothing is ever fully spent

Every trade leaves a remainder. A rounding, a residue, a fraction too small to name. The chain calls it dust and forgets it. For a long time, so did everyone else.

II

Then a star opened

Aster lit up and the remainders started to move — pulled together, given a shape, given a hood. What the market discarded stood up and looked back.

III

It carries a halo and a blade

The halo for where it came from. The blade for everything that gets in the way — spreads, slippage, hesitation. DUST does not negotiate with friction.

IV

Now it drifts, and it is not alone

No roadmap, no promises, no suits. A face for a place, and a crowd that decided it was theirs. That is the whole mechanism.
